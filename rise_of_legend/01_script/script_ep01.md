> Read the **PICTURE** column as what is on screen, **SOUND** as everything in the mix, and **NOTE**

---

## How this film is built

- **10 fps is the look.** Bodies animate on 3s, action and mouths on 2s, impacts on 1s. At 10 fps the
  held pose is the performance; if a drawing is wrong it is wrong for 300 ms.
- **One shot = one asset = one frame count.** The frame count in the header is law; the edit lives in
  `timeline.json`, not in a timeline app.
- **Silent-safe.** Cover the sound and the film must still make sense.
- **Only one graphic event in the whole film:** the title at S32.

## The five breaths (power rules)

The guardian holds **one element per breath**; changing element costs a full exhale. This is the whole
combat grammar, and it is the reason he can lose.

| Breath | Colour | Gives | Costs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Earth | ochre | armour, weight, catching | he cannot move fast |
| Water | teal | healing, flow | no offence |
| Fire | vermilion | one burst | it is gone in a breath |
| Air | silver | speed, dash | no defence while moving |
| Ether | violet | phasing, silence | he is not solid, cannot touch |

## ACT I — NIGHT

*We meet a man who restores paper, and a company that steals a stone.*

### S01 · 0:00.0 → 0:03.0 · 30 frames · cam hold

- **PICTURE** — Black frame. A thin gold rim appears at the top edge and widens: the eclipse. Wide of Nandipur from Yantra Hill — cooling towers, temple spires, one flyover, everything still. The sun is a black disc ringed in gold. Two birds cross the disc in two frames and are gone.
- **SOUND** — Low drone from frame 0. Wind, distant traffic. One temple bell under the whisper.
- **NOTE** — One drawing for the sky; only the birds animate (2-frame cycle). No camera move — the film opens on stillness.

### S02 · 0:03.0 → 0:05.6 · 26 frames · cam push

- **PICTURE** — High angle down a main street. The lights go out in a wave from the hill: sodium lamps first, then shopfronts, then apartment windows, block by block, each change held three frames. One scooter headlight keeps moving through the dark.
- **SOUND** — WHISPER (0:02.0–0:06.5): “जब आख़िरी मोहर टूटेगी, अंधेरा नाम लेगा।” — When the last seal breaks, darkness takes a name. Electrical hum dying, one dog barking twice.
- **NOTE** — Stepped 3% push (the builder applies it). Light changes on 3s — never on 1s, it strobes.

### S03 · 0:05.6 → 0:08.0 · 24 frames · cam hold

- **PICTURE** — Macro on Yantra Hill: five carved stone instruments in a row, one per element. The fifth — violet, ether — gains a hairline crack. A pinch of dust falls from it.
- **SOUND** — A stone tick on the crack; the drone drops a semitone.
- **NOTE** — The crack lands as a 3-frame white flash, then a 6-frame hold. This is the only magic in Act I.

### S04 · 0:08.0 → 0:14.0 · 60 frames · cam hold

- **PICTURE** — Hard cut to the museum basement: one lamp, glass cases, a ceiling fan, shelves of palm-leaf manuscripts. ARIN RATHORE (24, mustard kurta, ink-stained fingers) brushes a palm-leaf page at the restoration desk. He is calm. He is the least heroic person in the film.
- **SOUND** — Fan, brush, traffic far above. No score.
- **NOTE** — Hold the wide for the full 60 frames and let the audience settle. Introduce the 20-frame breath idle here — it is reused at S22 and S32.

### S05 · 0:14.0 → 0:19.0 · 50 frames · cam push

- **PICTURE** — Over his shoulder: the brush moves, and the ink beneath it does not obey — it crawls left, into a spiral, against the stroke.
- **SOUND** — Paper. One tabla hit at 0:15.0, exactly when the ink moves.
- **NOTE** — Ink on 2s (five drawings). The spiral is the five-petal shape, incomplete.

### S06 · 0:19.0 → 0:22.0 · 30 frames · cam hold

- **PICTURE** — His face reflected in the glass case. Behind the reflection, five faint coloured lines rise off the page — ochre, teal, vermilion, silver, violet — and bend toward him. The lamp flickers once.
- **SOUND** — Filament buzz, his breath, the fan dropping out.
- **NOTE** — Draw the reflection as a separate layer, offset 2 px. Wrong in a way the audience feels, not sees.

### S07 · 0:22.0 → 0:25.0 · 30 frames · cam push

- **PICTURE** — Insert: his right palm. A five-petal emblem burns in from the centre outward, one petal every four frames — ochre, teal, vermilion, silver, violet. He does not scream. He can't.
- **SOUND** — Five rising tones, one per petal, no music. Silence after the fifth.
- **NOTE** — Hot white core with an ochre bloom. The only insert in the film that gets a full-frame glow.

### S08 · 0:25.0 → 0:30.0 · 50 frames · cam pull

- **PICTURE** — The five breaths leave the mark as ribbons, spiral up his forearm, over the shoulder, and vanish. His sleeve smokes at the cuff. He gasps and grabs the desk.
- **SOUND** — The five tones stack into one chord, then cut to dry silence on the last frame.
- **NOTE** — Pull back 3%. Ribbons on 2s; the smoke is the only thing that moves on 1s.

### S09 · 0:30.0 → 0:35.0 · 50 frames · cam hold

- **PICTURE** — Hard cut: vault corridor, emergency red. A plasma cutter bites the door; sparks burst in 2-frame flashes. Three figures in survey-crew vests and helmets move with the calm of people doing a job. One lifts a brass-bound stone cube — the KEYSTONE — into a case.
- **SOUND** — Muted alarm, as if underwater. The cutter scream, boots on stone.
- **NOTE** — Faces never visible. The theft must look routine: this is a company, not a cult.

### S10 · 0:35.0 → 0:39.0 · 40 frames · cam hold

- **PICTURE** — ZOYA QURESHI (26, cyber-crime jacket over a kurta, badge swinging) steps out from behind a case, sidearm drawn, and shouts at the crew. She is alone. The crew does not stop.
- **SOUND** — ZOYA (0:38.0–0:40.0): “अरिन, भागो!” — Arin, run! Radio hiss, no reply.
- **NOTE** — Zoya never fires in this film. Finger off the trigger — that restraint matters later.

### S11 · 0:39.0 → 0:44.0 · 50 frames · cam push

- **PICTURE** — A survey drone clamps the pillar beside her and fires its bolts. The pillar tilts — slowly at first, then all at once — toward Zoya. She looks up.
- **SOUND** — Bolts pop, concrete groans. The score enters low for the first time.
- **NOTE** — Push 3%. The fall begins six frames before the cut: cut on the tilt, never on the impact.

### S12 · 0:44.0 → 0:48.0 · 40 frames · cam hold

- **PICTURE** — Arin runs in from frame-left, one hand out. He inhales — a hard, ugly, frightened breath — and the mark flares ochre. Plates of earth crawl up his forearm and over his shoulder in five steps.
- **SOUND** — One bass drop. His breath, doubled.
- **NOTE** — Armour grows on 2s, five drawings. Do not make it beautiful yet — it should look like it hurts.

### S13 · 0:48.0 → 0:52.0 · 40 frames · cam pull

- **PICTURE** — He catches the pillar across his shoulders. The floor cracks under him; dust sheets the whole frame.
- **SOUND** — Stone grind, sub-bass, then near-silence beneath the dust.
- **NOTE** — Impact = exactly one inverted frame, then a 6-frame hold. The dust wipe is the transition.

### S14 · 0:52.0 → 0:54.0 · 20 frames · cam hold

- **PICTURE** — The dust clears. Zoya stares at him. He stares at his own arm, terrified. Behind them the vault is empty — the crew and the keystone are gone. One petal of the mark has gone grey.
- **SOUND** — Dust settling, her breath, his heartbeat.
- **NOTE** — No dialogue. The thesis in one image: power without control, and a failure he caused.

## ACT II — FIRST LIGHT

*The lesson: one breath, one power, one choice.*

### S15 · 0:54.0 → 0:58.0 · 40 frames · cam hold

- **PICTURE** — A courtyard at first light. BADI AMMA (68, white sari, blind) circles Arin with a stick, eyes closed; marigolds and a chai kettle steam behind her. He is in a fresh kurta; the torn one is gone.
- **SOUND** — Birds, kettle, stick on stone. The Act I drone is gone — this scene has air.
- **NOTE** — Warmest palette in the film, and the only scene allowed to sit still and wide.

### S16 · 0:58.0 → 1:02.0 · 40 frames · cam hold

- **PICTURE** — Two panels. (1) Amma presses the stick to his chest; his earth stance holds and the stick bends. (2) Cut to his cut palm: he exhales teal and the cut closes.
- **SOUND** — Kettle whistle; a water swell under the teal. Amma's line starts here at 1:00.
- **NOTE** — Cut on the exhale. Her line deliberately bridges the cut into S17.

### S17 · 1:02.0 → 1:06.0 · 40 frames · cam hold

- **PICTURE** — Three quick panels: fire bursts from his palm and singes a marigold; an air-dash leaves a silver streak across the courtyard; ether flickers him half-transparent — she can see the kettle through him.
- **SOUND** — AMMA (1:00.0–1:04.0, bridging from S16): “एक साँस, एक शक्ति, एक चुनाव।” — One breath, one power, one choice. Kettle whistle, fire crack, a whip of air, then nothing under the ether.
- **NOTE** — Panels of ~13 frames each. The ether panel is the first time silence is used as a power.

### S18 · 1:06.0 → 1:10.0 · 40 frames · cam push

- **PICTURE** — He tries to hold two at once: ochre and teal auras climb his arms, beat against each other, flicker black, and die. He is left holding nothing.
- **SOUND** — Two tones beating faster and faster, then a wet pop and silence.
- **NOTE** — Play it straight — the failure costs something. The comedy belongs to her reaction, not his fall.

### S19 · 1:10.0 → 1:14.0 · 40 frames · cam hold

- **PICTURE** — The recoil throws him backward into a flowerbed; marigolds explode; he sits up with a flower on his head. Amma laughs — full, unguarded, slightly wicked. Hold 12 frames on her face.
- **SOUND** — Comedic tabla stinger, her laugh. The only laugh in the film.
- **NOTE** — No music under her laugh. Silence sells it.

### S20 · 1:14.0 → 1:17.0 · 30 frames · cam push

- **PICTURE** — He stands. Dust and gold leaf lift off the courtyard and spiral around him, pulled upward.
- **SOUND** — The drone returns, rising; air pulls.
- **NOTE** — Particles on 2s, one direction only. It should feel slow without actually being slowed down.

### S21 · 1:17.0 → 1:21.0 · 40 frames · cam hold

- **PICTURE** — The shawl unfolds from his shoulder like a manuscript page turning; brass filigree writes itself along its edge.
- **SOUND** — Paper turning, one metal chime.
- **NOTE** — One shape, never a cape. It moves only if he moves — no idle flutter.

### S22 · 1:21.0 → 1:26.0 · 50 frames · cam hold

- **PICTURE** — Low-angle hero shot. Five chest panels ignite left to right, one every six frames; the vented mask snaps closed over the lower half of his face. He holds for 12 frames.
- **SOUND** — Five stacked tones, one held note, then total quiet on the hold.
- **NOTE** — Poster frame. Draw it twice — pre-ignition and post-ignition — and hold the second. No camera move.

## ACT III — MORNING

*He chooses the crowd. The villain takes the hope.*

### S23 · 1:26.0 → 1:30.0 · 40 frames · cam hold

- **PICTURE** — Hard cut to rooftops at first light. SHUNYA stands on the next roof: bone-white armour, sound-dampening muffs, spear across his back, the keystone case at his feet. Around him the city goes quiet — birds, traffic, wind, all sucked out.
- **SOUND** — Ambience cuts on frame 1. Two breaths only, his and Arin's.
- **NOTE** — Eight frames of stillness before he moves. The silence is his character: never put score under him.

### S24 · 1:30.0 → 1:34.0 · 40 frames · cam push

- **PICTURE** — Arin takes the air breath and crosses the gap as a silver streak; tiles shatter where he lands.
- **SOUND** — Whip, tile crack, then the silence closes again.
- **NOTE** — 2-frame smear, land on 1s, hold 6. The only fast move in the film.

### S25 · 1:34.0 → 1:37.0 · 30 frames · cam hold

- **PICTURE** — Shunya thrusts the spear. Arin takes the ether breath and goes outline-only — violet lines, no fill — and the spear passes through the negative space where his chest was.
- **SOUND** — Absolute silence. One breath at the end, his.
- **NOTE** — Three frames outline-only, then solid. No whoosh. The absence is the effect.

### S26 · 1:37.0 → 1:40.0 · 30 frames · cam push

- **PICTURE** — Fire-palm: brass knuckles glow; he lands one clean hit on Shunya's guard. Three-frame inverted impact, then the two slide apart, both breathing hard.
- **SOUND** — Impact, then a ringing tone that fades under the next shot.
- **NOTE** — The only clean hit in the film. Do not repeat it — this fight is about escape, not victory.

### S27 · 1:40.0 → 1:44.0 · 40 frames · cam pull

- **PICTURE** — The fight has cracked the parapet. Below, a morning crowd — and Zoya, looking up — as debris falls toward them.
- **SOUND** — Crowd, cracking concrete, then the score cuts out entirely.
- **NOTE** — Pull back 3%. Faces are tiny, silhouettes only; their fear must read at a glance.

### S28 · 1:44.0 → 1:48.0 · 40 frames · cam hold

- **PICTURE** — Two-shot: the keystone case behind him at the roof's edge, the crowd below. Arin looks at the case. Then at the people. He turns away from the case.
- **SOUND** — TIMIR (V.O., off-screen, 1:44.0–1:47.5): “तुमने भीड़ चुनी… मैं उनकी उम्मीद लूँगा।” — You chose the crowd… I'll take their hope. One low hit under “hope”. No score.
- **NOTE** — He never speaks in this shot. The choice is physical: feet, shoulders, breath. Save his only line for episode 2.

### S29 · 1:48.0 → 1:52.0 · 40 frames · cam push

- **PICTURE** — Cut to Timir: a server-temple, racks breathing warm air, cable trays, a throne of them. He sits, the keystone case in his hand. His face is a field of static with gold cracks.
- **SOUND** — Server hum, compressor breath. No score at all under Timir.
- **NOTE** — Never show eyes. The static stutters on 3s — one drawing held three frames, then a new one.

### S30 · 1:52.0 → 1:56.0 · 40 frames · cam hold

- **PICTURE** — His wall: five sockets. The ether socket is dark — the stolen seal. The other four still hold light, and dim as he closes his fist. Outside, the sky over Nandipur dims with them.
- **SOUND** — Four descending tones, one per seal; a long exhale.
- **NOTE** — The four dim, they do not die. Arin keeps four petals. That is the series.

### S31 · 1:56.0 → 1:56.8 · 8 frames · cam hold

- **PICTURE** — Hard cut to black.
- **SOUND** — Nothing.
- **NOTE** — Exactly 8 frames. No fade, no sting.

### S32 · 1:56.8 → 2:00.0 · 32 frames · cam hold

- **PICTURE** — Frame 1 of the shot: RISE OF LEGEND slams in with a gold-leaf burst; “Ep. 1 — The Fifth Breath” settles beneath it. Two frames of gold dust, then dead still.
- **SOUND** — TITLE VO (1:57.2–1:59.8): “पाँच साँसें। एक रक्षक।” — Five breaths. One guardian. One impact, one held note, then cut.
- **NOTE** — Nothing animates after frame 26. The last frame is the thumbnail.

## Continuity bible

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| **The mark** | Right palm, five petals lit at S07. After the keystone is stolen, one petal goes grey (visible S14 onward) — four lit for the rest of the film, and in episode 2. |
| **Arin's sleeve** | Torn and smoking S09–S14. Fresh kurta from S15 — he went home. Never mix the two. |
| **The keystone** | Brass-bound stone cube, violet seam. S09 in the case, S28 at the roof's edge, S29 in Timir's hand, S30 in the ether socket. |
| **Screen direction** | The Grahan Sena always exits frame-right. Arin always enters from frame-left — he is always the one arriving to protect. |
| **Time of day** | Act I night → Act II pre-dawn → Act III first light. Timir's temple has no time of day: no windows, no clock, no shadows moving. |
| **Zoya's sidearm** | Drawn S10, holstered by S14, present but never fired at S27. She is the character who chooses not to shoot. |
| **Sound rule** | No score in Act I until S11. No score at all under Shunya or Timir. Silence is a character. |
| **The shawl** | Never reads as a cape. It moves only when he moves. It is a manuscript page, not fabric. |

## Animation & edit notes

1. **On 2s:** S05 ink, S07 burn, S08 ribbons, S12 armour, S17 panels, S20 particles, S24 dash.
2. **On 3s:** everything else, including all dialogue and both villain shots.
3. **On 1s (single frames):** S13 impact, S26 impact, S32 title arrival. Nowhere else.
4. **Impact frames:** one inverted frame at S13, three at S26. No other flashes in the film.
5. **Longest hold:** 60 frames on S04 — the only time the audience is allowed to relax.
6. **Shortest shot:** 8 frames (S31). Anything shorter stutters.
7. **Cut points:** every cut lands on a breath, a footfall, or a word — never on a round number.
8. **Title:** 8 frames of black (S31) then a 32-frame slam. Nothing animates after frame 26 of S32.
