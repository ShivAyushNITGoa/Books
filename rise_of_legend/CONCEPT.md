# RISE OF LEGEND — 2-minute pitch (10 fps animated short)

**Format:** 2D limited animation, 1920×1080, **10 fps**, 2:00 (1,200 frames), Hindi VO with English subtitles
**Genre:** Indian mythic-tech superhero · family-friendly YA
**Status:** original IP, legally distanced from *Shaktimaan* (see §7)
**Episode:** *Rise of Legend — Ep. 1: The Fifth Breath*

---

## 1. Logline

A 24-year-old manuscript restorer in the museum basement of Nandipur accidentally awakens the last of
the **Panch-Dwar** — five ancient seals holding back a faceless corporate sorcerer called **Timir**.
He can channel the five elements, but only **one breath at a time**, so every fight is a choice:
which power, and who gets saved.

> **Tagline:** *Five breaths. One guardian. One choice at a time.*

---

## 2. Why 10 fps is the right call (not a compromise)

10 fps is a **style decision** here, not a budget one. It gets you the "Indian comic panel come to
life" feel and halves the drawing count versus 24.

| Rule | Use |
|---|---|
| **Animation on 3s** (one drawing held 3 frames ≈ 3.3 drawings/sec) | dialogue, dread, Badi Amma's lesson, villain monologue |
| **Animation on 2s** (5 drawings/sec) | fight bursts, transformation, chases |
| **1s / 3-frame impact frames** | only for hits, seal cracks, title slam |
| **No slow camera pans** | at 10 fps a 6-second pan judders; use 8–12 frame holds and hard cuts instead |
| **Whip-pan = 2-frame smear** | cheaper and punchier than real motion blur |
| **3-frame colour inversion** | white-on-black impact frames, very "graphic novel" |
| **Looping 20-frame breathing cycle** | hero idle and villain presence, reusable all series |
| **Thick silhouette lines, flat fills, halftone shadows** | fine hatching shimmers badly at low fps |

## 3. The world (original, not the source show)

- **Nandipur** — a fictional tier-2 Indian city built around a hilltop observatory, the **Yantra Hill**.
- **Panch-Dwar** — five gateways (earth, water, fire, air, ether) sealed by an old astronomer guild.
  Each seal is a physical instrument, not a magic gem.
- **Panch Pran** — the "five breaths" technique. The guardian inhales on a five-beat cycle and holds
  **one element per breath**. Changing element costs a full exhale. This is the whole combat grammar:
  no flight-everywhere god-mode, just tactical switching.
- **The Grahan Sena** ("Eclipse Legion") — Timir's private security-cult, dressed as survey crews and
  disaster-response contractors. They steal infrastructure, not treasure.
- **Timir** — the Shadow Broker. A billionaire "energy and data" magnate whose face is always a
  shifting static mask with gold cracks. He doesn't want to rule the world; he wants to **own its
  certainty** — he feeds on fear, and sells safety. Modern, distinct from a cackling dark sorcerer.

## 6. Production sheet (how to actually make 1,200 frames)

- **Asset count:** 12 background plates (museum ×3, courtyard ×2, rooftop ×4, server-temple ×2, street ×1),
  6 characters, 5 element-VFX rigs, 1 reusable 20-frame breath cycle.
- **Drawing budget:** ~55% on 3s (≈220 unique drawings), ~40% on 2s (≈240), ~5% on 1s impact frames.
  Roughly **500 drawings + 12 plates** for the full 2 minutes.
- **Build order:** animatic at 5 fps stills → time the beat sheet → key poses → inbetweens →
  element VFX → grade (halftone + paper grain, no motion blur) → VO + mix.
- **Deliverables:** 1920×1080 10 fps h264 (CRF 20 for flat art), AAC 48 kHz; a 1080×1920 vertical
  cut for Shorts/Reels; a 4 s looping teaser (40 frames).
- **Sound:** original percussion + breath-driven drone. **Do not** reuse or imitate the source
  show's title track.
- **Tools:** OpenToonz / Krita / Blender Grease Pencil for the drawing; ffmpeg for the 10 fps encode.

### Note about this repo's builder

`build_video.py` is **hard-locked to 24 fps** (`FPS = 24`, `-r 24`, and `split_parts.py` /
`verify_parts.py` both assume 24). It also builds Ken Burns photo films, not frame-by-frame
animation. So this short is a **separate pipeline** — or I can add a `--fps` parameter to the
builder if you want it to assemble the animatic from stills at 10 fps.

## 7. Keeping it legally clean

Copyright protects **specific expression**, not the idea of a transforming Indian superhero. What we
deliberately removed:

| Source show element | Status here |
|---|---|
| Names: Shaktimaan, Gangadhar, Geeta, Kilvish, Ajooba | **Removed.** Entirely new cast list (§3) |
| "Om" activation / yogic siddhi order / guru lineage | **Replaced** with the five-breath, choice-based mechanic |
| Hero's day job as a newspaper photographer; journalist love interest | **Replaced** with museum restorer + cyber-crime officer |
| Costume colour scheme, chest emblem, cape silhouette | **Replaced** with indigo/brass, five-panel chest, vented mask, palm-leaf shawl |
| Villain as a dark-sorcery world-conqueror | **Replaced** with a surveillance/energy magnate who monetises fear |
| Catchphrases and the moral-lesson episode format | **Not used.** Serialised cliffhanger instead |
| Actor likeness, title song, episode plots | **Not used.** Original designs, original score, original story |

**Still to do before publishing:**
1. Trademark search for **"Rise of Legend"** — the phrase is crowded (games, books, fitness). Prefer a
   distinctive logo lockup and consider a unique subtitle, e.g. *"Rise of Legend: The Fifth Breath"*.
2. Never use "Shaktimaan" in titles, tags, thumbnails or SEO descriptions — that alone invites a
   takedown and can be evidence of intent to trade on the original.
3. Keep a short **character bible** with dated design files — it is your proof of independent creation.
4. Commission or license an original score; no AI voice-cloning of the original cast.
5. This is a production note, **not legal advice** — get an IP lawyer's read before monetising.

## 8. If you want a 20-second proof-of-concept first

Cheapest convincing test at 10 fps: **beats 1, 3, 5 and 8** (0:00–0:08, 0:22–0:30, 0:44–0:54,
1:14–1:26) = 440 frames ≈ 44 s of screen time. A moving animatic of just those four beats will tell
you whether the five-breath grammar reads on screen before you draw the other 760 frames.
