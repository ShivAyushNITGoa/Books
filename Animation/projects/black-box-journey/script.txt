Imagine designing the most important recorder on an airplane…

And then painting it bright orange.

Because that's exactly what engineers did.

You see, this little device records crucial flight data and cockpit conversations. And if a plane crashes, investigators need to find it.

But there's one problem.

Everyone calls it the black box.

So you'd expect it to be black, right?

Well, that would be a terrible idea.

After a crash, the recorder could end up buried in wreckage, hidden in forests, or lying deep underwater.

And a black box in all that darkness?

Good luck finding it.

So engineers chose a completely different colour.

Bright, highly visible orange.

They also added reflective markings to help search teams spot it, and underwater locator beacons to help find it beneath the sea.

And here's the funny part…

The device everyone calls a black box is actually designed to stand out like a traffic cone.

Because when an airplane disappears, the last thing investigators need…

is a recorder that's good at hiding.
